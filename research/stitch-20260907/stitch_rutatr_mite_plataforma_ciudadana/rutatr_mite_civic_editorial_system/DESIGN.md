## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, tranquil, and distinctly civic digital presence for independent public procedure navigation in Spain. Moving away from bureaucratic visual clichés, outdated governmental aesthetic traps, and pseudo-official heraldry, the design takes inspiration from high-grade investigative journalism, civic publishing houses, and quiet modernist editorial craft.

The brand persona is reliable, articulate, empathetic, and scrupulously clear. It speaks with the calm certainty of an expert archivist and the practical warmth of a helpful neighbor. Visual tension is deliberately modulated: generous bone-toned reading margins, balanced architectural proportion, and crisp typographic hierarchy replace dense administrative tables and confusing government layouts.

The design movement is **Contemporary Editorial**:
- **Literary Headlines & Functional Body**: Editorial serif titling creates trust, cadence, and human dignity, while an ultra-clean sans-serif provides immediate utility and maximum scan speed.
- **Organic Warmth over Bureaucratic Coldness**: Warm ivory and bone paper-grade backgrounds eliminate screen fatigue and bureaucratic impersonality.
- **Architectural Clarity**: Hierarchy is achieved through ruled dividers, disciplined grid spacing, and delicate borders rather than heavy drop shadows or synthetic containers.

## Colors

The color palette grounds civic utility in organic, calm tones that balance deep pine foliage, aged parchment surfaces, and understated functional accents.

### Palette Roles & Application
- **Primary Canvas & Surfaces**:
  - Base canvas is Warm Ivory (`#FAF7F2`), paired with Pure Bone White (`#FFFFFF`) for elevated cards, reading panels, and form fields.
  - Recessed backgrounds and structural bands use Muted Sand (`#F4EFEB` and `#F0ECE1`).
- **Inks & Contrasts**:
  - Primary ink is Deep Graphite (`#1F2421`), ensuring a strict 11.5:1 contrast against canvas tones.
  - Secondary metadata, captions, and reading aids employ Stone Ink (`#52575D`), sustaining 5.2:1 contrast against bone backgrounds.
  - Hairline framing and crisp structural rules use Pale Stone (`#D8D2C5`).
- **Brand & Semantic Accents**:
  - Pine Deep (`#1B4332`) and Forest Slate (`#2D6A4F`) govern interactive anchors, primary navigation badges, and critical pathways.
  - Chartreuse Tint (`#C2D66D` / `#D4E09B`) serves as a tactile, modern highlighter for live tags, category indicators, and status badges.
  - Terrestrial Amber (`#B07D38`) provides measured advisory warnings without visual hysteria.
  - Official Gazette Blue (`#2C5E7A`) cleanly identifies verified links to state gazettes (BOE, sedes electrónicas) without mimicking state crests.

## Typography

The typographic engine uses a deliberate optical tension between the literary warmth of `Newsreader` and the neutral clarity of `Inter`.

### Rationale & Scale Execution
- **Headlines (`Newsreader`)**: Evoke the authority of a curated civic gazette or cultural journal. Optical sizes and moderate weights (500-600) ensure readability without fragile thins. Negative tracking at larger scales preserves structural density.
- **Body & Administrative Data (`Inter`)**: Tall x-height, neutral aperture, and legible numeric tabular figures ensure long-form instructional copy, procedure checklists, timelines, and legal requirements remain instantly legible across screens.
- **Line Length**: Long-form editorial guides are capped at a maximum of `68ch` to maintain comfort during complex civic reading.

## Layout & Spacing

Layout in this design system follows an editorial 12-column responsive grid system defined by architectural order, rhythm, and expansive white space.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (>=1024px)**: 12 equal columns with `2rem` (32px) gutters, capped at a maximum container width of `76rem` (1216px). For procedure reading layouts, content divides into an asymmetrical 4/8 split: 3.5 columns for sticky navigational sidebars/step indexes and 8 columns for step-by-step procedural breakdown.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8 columns with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters. The sticky guide index reflows into a persistent, collapsible horizontal jump bar above the reading pane.
- **Mobile (<768px)**: 4 columns with `1rem` (16px) margins and gutters. Components stack into a singular vertical journey.

### Spatial Cadence
Vertical spacing pairs large section transitions (`space-3xl` / `space-4xl`) with dense, tightly grouped operational components (`space-xs` / `space-sm`), maintaining a distinct editorial cadence: expansive reading breath outside, dense procedural focus inside.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids fake glassmorphism, aggressive drop shadows, and heavy skew. Visual hierarchy is established via **tonal layering**, **structural rules**, and **ambient hairline depth**.

1. **Surface 0 (Canvas)**: Warm Ivory (`#FAF7F2`), flat, tactile, background plane.
2. **Surface 1 (Cards & Modules)**: Pure Bone (`#FFFFFF`) with a continuous `1px` border in Pale Stone (`#D8D2C5`).
3. **Surface 2 (Floating Search & Elevated Context)**: Pure Bone (`#FFFFFF`) paired with an ultra-diffused atmospheric shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(31, 36, 33, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(31, 36, 33, 0.03)`.
4. **Divider Philosophy**: Horizontal separations use crisp, single-pixel hairline borders (`#D8D2C5`). Vertical section indicators employ deliberate, tactile left-side strokes (`2px` solid `#2D6A4F` or `#D4E09B`).

## Shapes

The shape system operates at **Level 1 (Soft)**. It respects traditional printed press geometry while softening digital interactions just enough to feel humane and welcoming:

- **Standard Elements (inputs, buttons, cards)**: `0.25rem` (4px) radius (`rounded-sm` to default `rounded`).
- **Containers & Modal Drawers**: `0.5rem` (8px) radius (`rounded-lg`).
- **Interactive Badges & Category Chips**: Fully pill-shaped (`9999px`) purely for micro-level taxonomies (e.g., "Extranjería", "Hacienda", "Padrón"), preserving functional differentiation against rectangular content blocks.
- **Indicator Nodes**: Precision square or circular geometry (`24px` dimension) for numbered procedure steps.

## Components

### 1. Prominent Search Engine & Municipal Selector
- **Structure**: High-profile, card-level search bar composed of an editorial input alongside an inline municipality dropdown ("Toda España", "Madrid", "Barcelona", etc.).
- **Surface**: `#FFFFFF` with a `1px` border in `#D8D2C5`. Active focus shifts border to `#2D6A4F` with a soft outer ring `0 0 0 3px rgba(45, 106, 79, 0.15)`. No loud shadow animations.
- **Typography**: Search query input uses `Inter` body-lead font with muted charcoal placeholder (`#52575D`).

### 2. Category Chips & Badges
- **Form**: Pill-shaped badges with compact padding (`0.375rem 0.875rem`).
- **Rest State**: Tinted Sand (`#F0ECE1`) background with `#1F2421` text.
- **Selected State**: Pine Deep (`#1B4332`) with crisp `#FAF7F2` text.
- **Highlight State**: Subtle Chartreuse (`#D4E09B`) background with deep forest ink (`#0D2818`) for quick-access trends.

### 3. Procedure Step Cards & Timeline
- **Layout**: Linear vertical flow connected by a muted stone guideline (`1.5px` solid `#D8D2C5`).
- **Timeline Step Markers**: `28px` circular indicators with deep pine borders (`#2D6A4F`). Active/completed steps filled with `#1B4332` and white tabular numbers; pending steps filled with `#FAF7F2` and `#52575D` text.
- **Card Body**: Pure Bone White background, `1px` border in `#D8D2C5`, padded with `1.5rem`. Contains step title in `Newsreader` headline-sm, instructional sans-serif body copy, and actionable external links clearly flagged.

### 4. Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid Pine (`#1B4332`), `#FAF7F2` text, `0.25rem` border radius, padding `0.75rem 1.5rem`. Subtle hover to `#2D6A4F`.
- **Secondary / Ghost**: Transparent surface, `1px` solid `#1B4332` border, deep graphite text. Hover fills to `#F4EFEB`.
- **Official Source Action**: Soft Gazette Blue tint (`#2C5E7A` text, `#EBF2F7` background, `1px` solid `#BDD3E1`), accompanied by an external arrow indicator.

### 5. Verified Source & Advisory Banners
- **Verification Callout**: Never uses pseudo-police badges or seal stamps. Uses clean, left-bordered (`3px` solid `#2D6A4F`) cards on bone backgrounds with clear date-stamped verification copy ("Verificado con el BOE a 14 de marzo de 2024").
- **Amber Warning Callout**: Tinted Sand background (`#F9F6F0`) with a warm amber left rule (`#B07D38`) and ink in `#1F2421`, presenting administrative warnings calmly.

### 6. Interactive Form Controls (Checkboxes & Radio Buttons)
- **Geometry**: `18px` inputs with `0.125rem` radius for checkboxes; round for radio buttons.
- **Colorway**: Unchecked state displays a `#D8D2C5` stone border over pure white. Checked state transitions to `#1B4332` fill with high-contrast bone check icons.

### 7. Reading Indices (Sticky Table of Contents)
- **Behavior**: Sticks seamlessly to viewport top during scroll on desktop layouts.
- **Style**: Hairline list with dynamic left indicators. Active section title shifts from `#52575D` to bold `#1B4332` with a `2px` green rule snapping into alignment.
