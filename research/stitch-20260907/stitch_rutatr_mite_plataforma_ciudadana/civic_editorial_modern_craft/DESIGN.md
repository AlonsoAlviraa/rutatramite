## Brand & Style

This design system combines the authoritative weight of classic broadsheet publishing with the rigorous ergonomic precision of contemporary civic software. Tailored for complex navigational processes and institutional guidance, the aesthetic evokes clarity, unwavering institutional trust, and high-craft utility.

The experience moves entirely away from standard tech tropes—there are no nebulous gradient fields, no amorphous floating cards, and no glassmorphic blurs. Instead, the interface adopts a tactile broadsheet discipline: crisp ruled dividers, deliberate typographic hierarchy, warm paper-tinted substrates, and precise structural framing. It treats civic paperwork and municipal processes not as bureaucratic chores, but as structured, legible journeys through institutional territory.

The visual movement blends **Editorial Journalism** and **Architectural Utility**:
- **Literary Authority:** Editorial serif headlines anchor complex decision trees with clarity and quiet institutional confidence.
- **Architectural Scaffolding:** Hairline borders (`1px`) replace elevation layers, framing content through purposeful rule-lines and structural column bounds.
- **Utilitarian Energy:** A single high-visibility civic accent brings kinetic focus to urgent workflows, status states, and active directional decisions.

## Colors

The palette operates under rigorous restraint, balancing tactile newsprint surfaces against deeply saturated civic tones.

### Palette Mechanics
- **Primary (`#1B4332` - Deep Civic Pine):** Represents institutional gravitas, governance, and structural permanence. Used for primary interactive actions, high-level structural containers, and dominant masthead elements. A deeper variation (`#143527`) serves as the hover and active pressed state.
- **Secondary / Accent (`#A3CC3B` - Electric Civic Olive):** A sharp, energetic lime-olive tone chosen for precise visual punctuation. Used sparingly for status indicators, active step tags, current breadcrumbs, and high-priority callout rules. Never applied to large background areas.
- **Canvas (`#F7F4EE` - Warm Newsprint Paper):** The primary viewport background tone. Replaces harsh digital white with an organic, low-strain physical paper quality that grounds long reading sessions.
- **Surface Containers (`#FFFFFF` - Crisp Paper):** Reserved for active focus panels, document workspaces, form fields, and input cards to create clean architectural differentiation from the canvas.
- **Borders & Rules (`#E2DDD5` - Architectural Hairline):** Low-contrast structural lines that divide data modules, guide eyes along timelines, and establish borders without generating visual noise.
- **Typographic Neutrals:**
  - **Dominant Neutral (`#2B2F2C`):** Charcoal slate for primary reading text, headlines, and critical metric figures.
  - **Secondary Neutral (`#6B726D`):** Subdued stone for timestamps, field labels, peripheral metadata, and inactive step counters.

### Application Rules
Strictly avoid decorative color fills. Do not introduce purple, cyan, or multi-stop gradients. Color serves solely to organize data density and designate interactive status.

## Typography

The typographic strategy relies on high deliberate contrast between classical broadsheet prose (`Newsreader`) and sharp, legible interface scaffolding (`Plus Jakarta Sans`).

### Type Pairings & Roles
- **Newsreader:** Employed across display statements, stage titles, and section overviews. It brings literary cadence and weight, grounding municipal procedures in clear human narrative.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans:** Governs all functional interface components: form fields, navigation items, metadata tables, step flows, and data sheets. Its geometric legibility keeps UI panels calm and readable.
- **Display to Body Tension:** Dramatic scale transitions (such as pairing a `4.5rem` display headline directly above a `0.75rem` uppercase label) generate structural drama without cluttering the screen.

### Formatting Rules
- Headlines never use heavy bold weights; structural serifs achieve authority through scale rather than thickness.
- Use `subheading-editorial` (italicized Newsreader) exclusively for editorial commentary, document context, or secondary legal summaries.
- System labels, table column headers, and administrative tags must always utilize `label-structural` in uppercase tracking (`0.08em`).

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts are treated like an asymmetrical broadsheet page: structured, disciplined, and comfortable with whitespace.

### Grid Framework
- **Desktop (12 Columns):** Content conforms to a 12-column architectural grid with asymmetric distributions. Common splits include an 8/4 proportion (main procedural article on 8 columns, interactive navigation card index on 4) or a 3/6/3 split (step directory / active procedural canvas / metadata panel).
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** Collapses contextual panels into secondary tabs while keeping primary procedures spanning 6 or 8 columns.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** Single vertical stream anchored by fixed progress banners and sequential step unfolds.

### Spacing Philosophy
- Base layout increments adhere to an explicit 4px/8px modular rhythm.
- Generous structural vertical margins (`space-2xl` and `space-3xl`) isolate distinct workflow phases, preventing visual fatigue during regulatory submissions.
- Horizontal rules (`1px solid #E2DDD5`) establish structural separation between sections rather than arbitrary spacing blocks.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids simulated Z-axis drop shadows. Depth is communicated strictly through surface layering and physical framing.

### Planar Architecture
- **Layer 0 (Canvas):** The base `#F7F4EE` newsprint layer, supporting full-page scrolling and background layouts.
- **Layer 1 (Card/Container):** Pure `#FFFFFF` crisp surfaces bounded by a hairline `1px solid #E2DDD5` outline.
- **Layer 2 (Floating Status / Floating Shelves):** High-priority sticky bars or contextual drawers use a solid white surface with a fine double border: an inner `1px solid #E2DDD5` encased by a razor-thin structural shadow: `0 2px 0 0 rgba(43, 47, 44, 0.04), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(43, 47, 44, 0.06)`. No colored, blurry, or ambient drop shadows are permitted.

### The Architectural Rule
When elements overlap or require separation, use solid `1px` lines instead of elevation. A persistent horizontal or vertical hairline creates boundary and clarity without visual clutter.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a low-radius, structured profile (`roundedness: 1`). Elements feel pressed, cut, and architectural rather than soft or pill-shaped.

### Radius Application
- **Standard Corners (`0.25rem` / 4px):** Applied to inputs, buttons, chips, nested document previewers, and badges.
- **Structural Containers (`0.375rem` / 6px):** Maximum boundary radius applied only to top-level panels, master modals, and primary workflow frames.
- **Circular Elements:** Reserved solely for standalone icon buttons, numeric step badges (`1`, `2`, `3`), and radio indicators. General containers or buttons must never use pill borders.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep Civic Pine (`#1B4332`) fill with crisp white text, `border-radius: 4px`, padding `10px 20px`. Typography is `Plus Jakarta Sans` 600 weight. Hover state deepens to `#143527` with a subtle `transform: translateY(-1px)`. Active state settles flat at `0px`.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background transparent, boundary `1px solid #2B2F2C`, text `#2B2F2C`. Hover initiates a light tint of `#1B4332` at 4% opacity.
- **Civic Action (Accent):** Solid `#A3CC3B` background with `#1B4332` high-contrast typography. Reserved for concluding steps, document downloads, or critical validation triggers.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Background `#FFFFFF`, wrapped in a crisp `1px solid #E2DDD5` hairline, corner radius `4px`.
- Input text uses `body-default` in `#2B2F2C`.
- **Focus State:** Hairline switches to solid `#1B4332` with an additional `1px` outer ring (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px #1B4332`). Soft color glows are forbidden.
- Field labels are strictly styled in `label-structural` uppercase stone (`#6B726D`), placed `6px` above the input bounding box.

### Chips & Badges
- Compact rectangular profiles (`padding: 3px 8px`, `border-radius: 3px`).
- **Informational:** `#F7F4EE` background with `#6B726D` text and a fine `#E2DDD5` border.
- **Active Step / Verified:** Tinted `#A3CC3B` with deep pine text (`#1B4332`), weight 600.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Square geometry (`4px` radius for checkboxes, full circle for radios), sized at `18px × 18px`.
- Border `1.5px solid #2B2F2C` against `#FFFFFF`.
- Checked state fills with `#1B4332`, bearing a sharp white geometric checkmark or white inner circular pip.

### Lists & Step Flow Trackers
- Procedural step trackers feature vertical hairline dividers (`1px solid #E2DDD5`).
- Active steps display a solid `#1B4332` circle with a sharp white numeral. Pending steps use an unfilled `#E2DDD5` border with stone-toned numbering.
- Completed steps feature an `#A3CC3B` pip with a pine checkmark.

### Cards & Document Workspaces
- Constructed using a pure `#FFFFFF` background set against the `#F7F4EE` page canvas.
- Framed with an explicit `1px solid #E2DDD5` boundary.
- Internal headers include an integrated bottom hairline divider, separating top-level editorial metadata from interactive body content.
